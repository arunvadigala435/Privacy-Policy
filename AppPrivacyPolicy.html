Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the information in the app may not have legal sanctity. Users are advised to verify important information with the appropriate authorities. APCFSS is not responsible for the accuracy or completeness of content provided by third parties.

Information We Collect

User-provided Information

The application may collect information you provide when you download or use the app. The app may request personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Location
  • Contact number

Some features rely on third-party services (e.g., Google). These third-party services may collect information about you; please review their privacy policies for more details.

Automatically Collected Information

The application may automatically collect technical and usage information including but not limited to:

  • Device type and model
  • Device unique identifier
  • Mobile operating system
  • App usage and analytics

If enabled, the app may use GPS or similar technologies to determine your approximate or precise location. Location data may be shared with authorities if required for service-related actions.

Users may disable location services through device settings, but certain app features may not function correctly without location access.

Passwords

Passwords are encrypted and stored securely.

Transactional Information

Permissions

To provide features as per the department’s objectives, the application may collect, with the user’s prior permission, location and camera access for registration and identification purposes during service delivery. No other device features are accessed or required.

Data Privacy & Protection

The app uses standard security measures to protect confidentiality. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in secure, department-managed environments.

Cookies

The application may use cookies or similar local storage mechanisms. These are used solely for internal functionality and not for tracking purposes.

Data Retention

User-provided data is retained on the device for as long as the app remains installed. Some data may be essential for core functionality.

Misuse by Non-targeted Users

The app is intended for targeted users only. Misuse should be prevented by the app owner.

Security

We employ reasonable security safeguards; however, no system can guarantee absolute security. Users should be aware of residual risks.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the app indicates acceptance of any changes. Please review this policy regularly.

Your Consent

By using the application, you consent to this Privacy Policy and its future amendments.
